Privacy Policy

App: Riddle Rush Auto Poster  |  Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

This application accesses the following TikTok account data via the TikTok API, with your explicit authorization:

  • Basic profile information (display name, open_id) — used to confirm account identity
  • OAuth access tokens — stored locally on your device to enable posting

2. How We Use Your Information

Data is used solely to post video content to your TikTok account on your behalf. No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties.

3. Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally in a JSON file on your device (tiktok_token.json). No data is stored on remote servers.

4. Data Retention

Tokens are retained until you delete the file or revoke access in your TikTok account settings at TikTok Privacy Settings.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke this app's access at any time via TikTok Settings → Privacy → Apps and websites that can access your account.
